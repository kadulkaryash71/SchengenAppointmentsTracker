Terms & Privacy Notice

Dublin Schengen Appointment Alerts

Independence notice. This service is run independently and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to VFS Global, any embassy, or any consulate. It cannot guarantee that an alert reflects real-time availability — always confirm on the official VFS portal before relying on it.

What this service does

We check publicly visible Schengen visa appointment pages on VFS on a best-effort basis, roughly every few minutes, and send you a message when a new appointment slot or waitlist opening appears for the countries you've chosen.

What we collect

  • Your email address
  • Your phone number and/or Telegram username, if you provide them, for WhatsApp or Telegram alerts
  • The countries and visa type you ask us to track
  • The date and time you gave consent to be contacted

Why we collect it

Solely to send you appointment alerts and to let you manage your subscription. We don't sell, rent, or share your details with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as your alert subscription is active, and delete them once you unsubscribe or ask us to remove your data.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your data, or unsubscribe entirely, at any time by emailing your-email@example.com. We'll action requests as quickly as we can.

No guarantee

Appointment monitoring depends on VFS's own website staying accessible and unchanged. We do our best to keep alerts accurate and timely, but we can't guarantee a slot will still be available by the time you try to book it, or that our monitoring will catch every change.

Changes to this notice

If this notice changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
